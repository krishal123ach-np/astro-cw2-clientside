About AstroLaunch Expeditions

Our Mission Statement

We think distance shouldn't decide who gets to explore. AstroLaunch exists to make crewed trips to other worlds something you can actually book, not just something governments do.

Our Story

AstroLaunch started as a small research team studying which nearby planets and moons could realistically support a crewed landing. Once our first rocket passed certification, we moved from research into actually flying missions — starting with Mars, then expanding to Europa and Kepler-186f.

Meet the Crew

Every mission runs through the same three teams: Flight Systems designs and maintains the rockets, Planetary Science builds the habitability checks run on every landing, and Ground Control handles bookings and keeps in touch with crews before and during launch.
