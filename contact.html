Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior spaceflight experience?

No. Every crew goes through an 8-week pre-flight training program with our ground team before departure, so no experience is required to book.

Can I change my destination after booking?

Yes, up to 60 days before your launch date, depending on rocket and crew availability. Get in touch through this page and we'll check what's possible.
