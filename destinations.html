Our Destinations

Mars

Our most-flown route. Mars has a 4-month transit and existing surface infrastructure from earlier missions, which makes it the usual first pick for new crews. Gravity sits around 0.38g, average temperature is -63°C, and the atmosphere is thin CO2 — you'll need a suit outside the base. Habitability score: 58%.

Europa

Jupiter's icy moon. Longer trip at around 7 months, and much colder — averaging -160°C, with only trace oxygen in the atmosphere. What makes it worth it is the subsurface ocean, giving it the best odds in the solar system of finding liquid water. Habitability score: 41%.

Kepler-186f

Our longest and most ambitious route, this exoplanet sits in its star's habitable zone. Gravity is close to Earth's at 1.1g, average temperature is around -5°C, and the atmosphere is breathable with supplemental equipment. Water is confirmed present. Habitability score: 82% — the highest in our current catalog.
